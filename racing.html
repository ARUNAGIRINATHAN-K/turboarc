How to Play

Master Apex Circuit in 3 Steps

Dynamic Racing Line

Green zones are full throttle. Yellow signals setup for an upcoming turn. Red shows hard braking zones, backed by 150m, 100m, and 50m trackside boards.

Drift Combos

Hold SPACE while turning into a corner to kick out the rear tyres. Maintain angle and speed to build up to a 5x combo, then release cleanly to bank points. Spinning out or slamming barriers breaks your combo.

Nitro Boost

Charge Nitro (0–100%) by drifting, hitting clean corners, and overtaking rivals (+22%). Press SHIFT to surge above 300 km/h with exhaust flames. Empty tanks lock until 15% recharged.
